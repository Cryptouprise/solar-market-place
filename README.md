# Solar Appointment Marketplace

## Overview
This marketplace platform is designed for selling **solar preset appointments**, not solar panels. We connect homeowners interested in solar energy with qualified solar installers through scheduled appointments.

## Business Model
- **Product**: Pre-set solar consultation appointments
- **Service**: Connecting homeowners with solar installation professionals
- **Delivery**: Scheduled appointments at homeowner locations

## Key Features

### House Imagery
For each appointment listing, we require a **front-facing photograph of the house** obtained from Google photos/Street View. This helps:
- Solar installers prepare for the consultation
- Provide accurate preliminary assessments
- Ensure proper appointment scheduling
- Identify property characteristics before the visit

### Appointment Process
1. Homeowner requests solar consultation
2. Property front image is captured from Google Street View
3. Appointment is scheduled with qualified solar installer
4. Installer reviews property image before visit
5. On-site consultation takes place

## Image Requirements
- **Source**: Google Street View / Google Photos
- **View**: Front-facing view of the property
- **Purpose**: Pre-appointment property assessment
- **Quality**: Clear, recent imagery preferred

## Important Note
This platform does **NOT** sell solar panels directly. We facilitate appointments between homeowners and solar installation professionals.
